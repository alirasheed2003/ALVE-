The Science

How capsule creams
actually work.

The full story of the capsule-in-cream system — what it is, why freshness is the mechanism and not the marketing, and what happens in the ten seconds between the jar and your face.

The problem with traditional creams

A conventional face cream is a finished blend: actives, emulsifiers, water and oils all combined at the factory, then sealed and shipped. From that day forward, every ingredient inside is exposed to every other ingredient — and to air, light and temperature swings — for months on a shelf and months more on your counter.

Some of skincare's most valuable actives are also its most delicate. Vitamin C oxidizes. Peptides degrade. Antioxidants spend themselves fighting the environment inside the jar instead of working on your skin. By the time you reach the bottom of a traditional cream, you're often applying a formula that has quietly lost strength.

The whole idea behind ALVEÉ is simple: keep the actives separated until the moment of use. Freshness isn't a word on the label — it's the mechanism.

The dual-texture system

Open any jar in the Capsule Cream Series and you'll find two formulas deliberately kept apart: visible essence capsules — soft, color-coded spheres — suspended in a crystal-clear cream base.

The capsule carries the hero actives: Copper Tripeptide-1 and PDRN in the blue jar, a fresh dose of Vitamin C in the golden capsule, Matcha Extract in the green one, Astaxanthin in the vivid orange sphere. Each is a small, protected reservoir — concentrated and shielded until you decide it's time.

The clear cream base is the delivery vehicle: a lightweight aqua-gel of Sodium Hyaluronate, Beta-Glucan, Panthenol and Ceramide NP that cushions skin, supports the moisture barrier and carries the capsule's payload evenly across your face.

The burst

Scoop a portion of cream together with a capsule. Press and blend on your palm. In a few seconds the sphere gives way and melts — releasing its concentrated essence into the fresh cream around it. You are mixing a fresh dose of skincare at the moment of application, the way a barista pulls an espresso shot instead of pouring day-old coffee.

Frequently asked

Do I use the capsule and cream together or separately?

Always together. Take an appropriate amount of cream along with a capsule, blend on your palm until the sphere fully melts, then apply. The cream is designed as the carrier for the capsule's actives.

How often should I use it?

Morning and evening, after cleansing and toning. For daytime use, always finish with sunscreen.

Will the spheres feel grainy?

No — the spheres are soft cream, not beads. They melt within seconds of blending and leave only a dewy, non-greasy finish.

Is it suitable for sensitive skin?

The formulas are dermatologist tested and built with soothing agents like Allantoin and Panthenol. The Botanical Green (Centella Comfort) jar is designed specifically for fragile, reactive skin. As with any skincare, patch test before first use.

Can I layer it with my other products?

Yes. The lightweight gel-cream absorbs fast and layers cleanly under makeup and SPF, and over serums and toners. It slots into the moisturizer step of any routine.

About ALVEÉ

Serious skincare.
Unserious devotion to it.

ALVEÉ Skin Science was built on one conviction: the most advanced actives in the world are wasted if they arrive at your skin already tired.

We started ALVEÉ after asking a simple question that most of the beauty industry avoids: how fresh is a cream, really, by the time it reaches your face? The honest answer — months old, sometimes years — became our founding problem to solve.

The answer became our obsession: capsule-in-cream technology that keeps hero actives sealed inside visible essence spheres, suspended in a crystal-clear base, until the moment you blend them on your palm. Every application is, in a real sense, freshly made.

Today the Capsule Cream Series spans nine color-coded formulas — from blue GHK-Cu barrier care to the gold-flecked Golden Glow — alongside our whipped tallow balm, beauty-from-within capsules, and the tricolor primer. Each one is dermatologist tested, cruelty free, and formulated without parabens, mineral oil, artificial fragrance, artificial color or alcohol.

We're an independent brand, operated from Florida and sold to customers across the US and worldwide through Amazon. No corporate parent, no shortcuts — just an obsession with the ten seconds between the jar and your glow.
